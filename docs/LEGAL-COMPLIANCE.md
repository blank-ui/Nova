# Legal and compliance

Public legal routes are initial drafts and clearly labeled for review. They are not legal advice and must not be published as final policy without a qualified review for the operating entity, markets served, privacy/data protection obligations, consumer law, tax, accessibility commitments, and actual vendors/data flows.

Before launch, provide the legal entity name, address/contact, governing law, retention and deletion schedules, processors/subprocessors, cookie/analytics inventory, consent and rights workflows, signed service terms, scoped refund/cancellation rules, and accessibility feedback contact. Ensure website claims and portfolio permissions are accurate.

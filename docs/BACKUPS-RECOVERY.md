# Backups and recovery

Back up MySQL independently from the hosting account, encrypt backups, restrict access, and set retention based on business and legal needs. Back up private files with checksums and matching metadata. Document the database and file restore order, secrets required, DNS/process recovery, and contact escalation.

Initial planning targets are 4–8 hours for restoration and no more than 24 hours of ordinary record loss; these are targets only until measured. Perform and record an isolated restore test before production, then repeat on a schedule. A successful backup job alone does not verify recoverability.
